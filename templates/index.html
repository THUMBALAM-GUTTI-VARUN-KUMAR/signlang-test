Gesture Finger Shortcuts & Hand Guide
Which Fingers to Use
➕ MORE 2 Hands
Fingers: All 5 fingertips of both hands clustered into tapered points (O-tips).
Action: Tap fingertips of both hands together in the center.
📖 BOOK 2 Hands
Fingers: Flat open hands (all 5 fingers straight).
Action: Touch pinky sides together, then open upward like opening a book.
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 FAMILY 2 Hands
Fingers: Both hands in F-shape (Thumb + Index touch in circle, Middle/Ring/Pinky spread upright).
Action: Bring both F-hands together until index/thumb circles touch.
🏠 HOUSE 2 Hands
Fingers: Flat hands (all 5 fingers straight).
Action: Angle hands diagonally and touch fingertips to form a roof (^).
🆘 HELP 2 Hands
Fingers: Dominant = Thumbs-Up fist (👍) | Non-dominant = Flat palm (🖐️).
Action: Place the Thumbs-Up fist firmly on top of the flat palm.
💼 WORK 2 Hands
Fingers: Both hands closed in Fists (✊ ✊).
Action: Tap dominant fist on top of non-dominant wrist/fist twice.
🤝 FRIEND 2 Hands
Fingers: Both Index fingers curved/hooked (☝️ ☝️), other fingers in fists.
Action: Lock/hook both curved index fingers together in the center.
🎮 PLAY 2 Hands
Fingers: Thumb + Pinky extended on both hands (Dual Shaka 🤙 🤙).
Action: Rotate/shake both wrists back and forth playfully.
🙏 THANK YOU 2 Hands
Fingers: Both flat hands with all 5 fingers straight and pressed together.
Action: Press both palms together in front of chest (Namaste/Pray).
💊 TABLET 1 Hand
Fingers: Dominant Thumb + Index tips pinched together (Pill pinch), others curled in.
Action: Hold pill pinch steadily in front of camera.
🍲 FOOD 1 Hand
Fingers: All 5 fingertips of dominant hand gathered/clustered into a tapered point.
Action: Tap gathered fingertips toward your mouth.
💧 WATER 1 Hand
Fingers: Index, Middle, and Ring straight up (W shape), Thumb holds Pinky.
Action: Hold W-handshape upright in camera.
☝️ ME / I 1 Hand
Fingers: Dominant Index finger straight up, other 4 fingers in a fist.
Action: Point index finger straight up or toward your chest.
🤟 I LOVE YOU 1 Hand
Fingers: Thumb, Index, and Pinky extended; Middle & Ring curled into palm.
Action: Face palm forward toward camera.
🤙 CALL ME 1 Hand
Fingers: Thumb and Pinky extended (Phone shape), 3 middle fingers curled.
Action: Hold phone handshape upright in camera.
✌️ PEACE 1 Hand
Fingers: Index and Middle fingers extended in a wide V shape.
Action: Hold V-shape facing camera.
👌 OK 1 Hand
Fingers: Thumb + Index tips touch in a circle; Middle, Ring, Pinky straight up.
Action: Hold circle handshape upright.
👋 HELLO 1 Hand
Fingers: All 5 fingers extended open wide with thumb spread out.
Action: Open palm facing forward toward camera.
