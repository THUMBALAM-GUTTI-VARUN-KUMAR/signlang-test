How SignAI Operates

Experience frictionless translation in three simple steps.

01

Position Your Hand

Open the camera workspace. The system detects your hand bounding box with high frame-rate tracking.

02

Neural AI Inference

The 21 landmark skeleton is isolated and classified across 8 neural subgroup branches in real time.

03

Instant Speech & Text

Words are assembled, spell-checked, polished, and spoken out loud via text-to-speech voice synthesis.
